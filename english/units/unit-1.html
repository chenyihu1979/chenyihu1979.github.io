I suddenly remembered how much I loved this art.
I posted my doubts online: “Well, should I continue?”
To my surprise, the post was flooded with warm answers.
Many people expressed their love for the art and encouraged me to hold on.
A truth hit me – it was my responsibility to keep the art alive because the love for puppets was in my blood.
